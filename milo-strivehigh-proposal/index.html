FAQ

Questions for the team

Is this HIPAA compliant?

Yes. We execute a Business Associate Agreement before any data transfer. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, processed in SOC 2 certified infrastructure, and never used to train AI models. Full compliance documentation available on request.

We were looking at Power BI or Tableau. Why not just go with those?

Power BI and Tableau are excellent for pre-built dashboards — and you may still want them for certain views. But they require someone to build every report before anyone can use it. When your clinical director needs a quick utilization check mid-meeting, or your CEO wants a cash flow projection that accounts for billing week lag, those tools can't answer on the fly. Milo handles the questions that come up between dashboards.

What about our billing week structure? Thursday to Wednesday doesn't work in most tools.

Milo's knowledge base is configured with your specific definitions during setup — including your Thursday-to-Wednesday billing week, your KPI formulas, your CPT code mappings. When someone asks about "last week," Milo knows that means Thursday to Wednesday, not Sunday to Saturday.

How long does setup take?

For your EMR data: typically 2–3 days from credentials to first queries. The 3-week POC starts there so you get value immediately. Payroll and additional sources are connected in parallel during the pilot.
